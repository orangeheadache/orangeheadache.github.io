Handdrawn, playful, and fun!


I wanted my logo to be playful and fun because I feel that that's an important part of my personality. Instead of using traditional fonts, I went for a handdrawn style that mimics the way I write my name in everyday life! It's a representation of something that I actually do to keep little things fun and the way that the letters in my name play together.
