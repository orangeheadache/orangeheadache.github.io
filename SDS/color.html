Approved pairings

WCAG 2.2 AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (24px, or 18.66px bold) and for interface parts like borders and focus rings. Dark Orange was darkened from the original Figma #F29100 to #B75C00 so it passes 4.5:1 as text on White. Warm Orange is light, so it always carries black text.
