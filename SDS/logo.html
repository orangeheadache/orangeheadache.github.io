Foundations

Logo

Two versions of the logo: a reverse version for the dark navbar and a black and white version for light backgrounds.

Versions

Reverse logo

For dark backgrounds such as the Navbar Dark (#071500) bar. Figma size 253 × 238.

Black and white logo

For white backgrounds, single-color printing and places where color isn't available. Figma size 250 × 234.

Usage

Do

  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least 10px at nav size, and about a quarter of the logo's width at larger sizes.
  • Use the reverse logo on Navbar Dark and the black and white logo on White.
  • Scale proportionally. The nav version is 61 × 60.

Don't

  • Don't place either version on Dark Orange or Warm Orange.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, recolor or add shadows.
  • Don't go below 40px tall; the inner layers stop reading.

Accessibility

  • When the logo stands alone, give it alt text naming the site, for example alt="Al Rajala portfolio".
  • When the logo sits next to the site name or inside a home link that already has text, use alt="" so screen readers don't say it twice.
  • The logo needs at least 3:1 contrast with its background. Both approved backgrounds meet this.
